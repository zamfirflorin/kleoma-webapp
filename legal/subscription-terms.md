## How to cancel

Cancel in your store account, not in our app:

- **iPhone and iPad:** Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Kleoma, Cancel subscription.
- **Android:** Google Play, Profile icon, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Kleoma, Cancel subscription.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting your Kleoma account does **not** cancel a subscription.
