## The website

**kleoma.com does not set cookies and does not use similar technologies to track you.**

- We do not use analytics, advertising or social-media scripts.
- Fonts and icons are served from our own site, so your browser does not contact Google or another font provider.
- We do not store anything in your browser's local storage or session storage.
- Because of this, the website does not show a cookie banner: there is nothing that needs your consent.

Like every website, our servers and our delivery network (Cloudflare) receive your IP address and basic request details when you load a page. This is needed to deliver the page and to protect it from abuse. Logs are kept for a short time, about 30 days. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy). [VERIFY: confirm that the Cloudflare plan in use does not set a bot-management or security cookie on kleoma.com; check the live response headers before publishing.]

## The app

The app is not a website and does not use browser cookies. It does store information on your device so it can work offline and remember your choices: your settings, a cache of lessons and audio, and your learning progress. If the app shows ads, Google AdMob may use an advertising identifier on your device. That identifier is only used for personalised ads with your consent, and you can reset or turn it off in your phone settings. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Consent and age confirmation](/consent).

## If this changes

If we add a feature to the website that needs cookies or similar technologies (for example analytics), we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it, before anything is set.
