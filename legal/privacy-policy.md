## The short version

- You create an account with an email address, Google or Facebook. We store your language choices, your learning progress and your daily-practice flame, so the app can pick up where you left off on any device.
- We do **not** sell your data, and we do not use your learning data for advertising.
- If the app shows ads, Google AdMob may use an advertising identifier. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this happens only after you agree in a consent form, and on iPhone only if you allow tracking. You can change this at any time.
- The website uses no cookies and no analytics.
- You can export or delete your data in the app (Settings, Data and privacy), or by writing to us.

## Advertising and tracking

If the app shows ads, they are provided by Google AdMob.

- **Consent form.** In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (the Google User Messaging Platform) asks you whether ads may be personalised. You can open it again in Settings, Privacy options.
- **iPhone.** Apple asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, the app does not receive your advertising identifier and shows non-personalised ads.
- **What is never used for ads.** Your learning progress, your language choices and your messages to us are not shared with advertisers.

## Who receives your data

We use service providers (processors) who handle data only on our instructions. The current list, with their purpose and location, is on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors). The main ones are Google (sign-in, database, hosting, ads), Cloudflare (delivery and protection of the service), RevenueCat (purchases), and Apple and Google for the app stores and payments.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it. If we ever merge with or are acquired by another company, personal data may pass to it, and we will tell you before it is governed by a different policy.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- **have it corrected** if it is wrong;
- **have it deleted** ("right to be forgotten");
- **restrict** or **object to** certain processing, in particular processing based on our legitimate interests;
- **receive your data** in a portable format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting what happened before.

The quickest way is in the app: Settings, Data and privacy, where you can export or delete your data. You can also write to {{contact.privacyEmail}}. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We answer within one month.

You may complain to a data protection authority. Ours is {{company.supervisoryAuthority}}; you can also contact the authority where you live or work.

## Children

Kleoma is not for children. You must be at least {{legal.minimumAge}} years old to use the app, and the app asks you to confirm it. We do not knowingly collect data from younger people. If you think a child has given us data, write to {{contact.privacyEmail}} and we will delete it.

## Automated decisions

The app chooses which words and phrases to play next from your learning progress. This is personalisation of the study order. It has no legal or similarly significant effect on you, and you can always pick content yourself.

## Security

We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, separate credentials for each service, and network protection. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

## California and other US states

We do not sell personal information. If personalised ads are shown, some US state laws call the use of an advertising identifier "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can switch this off in Settings, Privacy options, or in your device settings. You may ask to access, correct or delete your data at {{contact.privacyEmail}}, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.
