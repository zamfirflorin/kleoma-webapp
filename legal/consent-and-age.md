This page describes every choice the Kleoma app asks you to make, so you know what you agreed to and how to change it. Some of these are required to use the app; the rest are optional and off until you turn them on.

## What the app asks, and when

| When | What you are asked | Required? | What we record | How to change it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First launch, before you can continue | Accept the [Terms of Service](/terms) and acknowledge the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) | **Yes.** Without it we cannot provide the service. | The version of each document you accepted and the time | You can read both any time in Settings. If a document changes in a way that matters, the app asks you again. |
| First launch | Confirm that you are at least {{legal.minimumAge}} years old | **Yes** | The version of the age confirmation and the time | Delete your account if you no longer wish to be bound. |
| First launch | Allow optional **analytics** | No | Your yes or no | Settings, Data and privacy |
| First launch | Allow **marketing** messages | No | Your yes or no | Settings, Data and privacy, and the unsubscribe link in any message |
| After the first screen (EEA, UK, Switzerland) | **Ad consent form** from Google: whether ads may be personalised and which partners may use your data | No | Google stores your choice | Settings, Privacy options, or the same form again when Google asks |
| After the ad consent form (iPhone and iPad) | **Apple's tracking prompt**: whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites | No | Apple stores your choice | iPhone Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking |
| When you turn on reminders | **Notifications** permission from your phone | No | Your phone stores your choice | Phone settings, Notifications |

## What we record about your consent

When you accept the terms the app stores a consent record in your profile:

- the version of the Terms of Service you accepted,
- the version of the Privacy Policy you acknowledged,
- the version of the age confirmation,
- the time you accepted,
- your choice on analytics and marketing.

We keep this so we can show, if asked, what you agreed to and when. The time is taken from your device.

## Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw an optional consent at any time in the app, and it applies from then on. It does not change anything we did before you withdrew. Withdrawing a **required** acceptance means you can no longer use the service; you can delete your account in the app.

## When a document changes

Each legal document has a version, shown at the top of its page (for example 2026-10). When the Terms or the Privacy Policy change in a way that matters, we raise the version and the app asks you to accept the new version before you continue. You can see every current version at [kleoma.com/legal/versions.json](/legal/versions.json) and the full list at [kleoma.com/legal](/legal).

## The website

This website does not set cookies or load third-party trackers, so it does not ask for cookie consent. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
